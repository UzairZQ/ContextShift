# ContextShift: The Neural Life Operating System

> **"Your digital space should be a living partner, not a static container."**

ContextShift is an **Agentic Environment** designed to mirror your cognitive state. Powered by **Gemini 2.0 Flash** and a proprietary **GenUI** engine, it rebuilds itself on-demand to match your focus.

---

## ⚡ Neural Connectivity: AI status in Real-Time
ContextShift isn't just "connected"—it breathes.
- **Neural Pulse**: The top-right status indicator glows **Neural Green** when the JARVIS backend is synchronized and active.
- **Resilient Heartbeat**: A 10-second backend pulse ensures that if JARVIS goes offline, the UI gracefully shifts to protect your workflow with local fallbacks.
- **Fast-Fail Stability**: Intelligent 15s timeouts prevent dashboard hangs, ensuring your core productivity data (tasks/habits) always loads instantly.

## ✨ The GenUI Innovation: Ephemeral Intelligence
- **Proactive Cards**: JARVIS generates interactive modules (Workout Plans, Strategic Routines, Advice) based on your live context.
- **Backlog Infection**: AI-suggested items are fully interactive. Review, edit, and "infect" your permanent task backlog with AI insights in a single tap.
- **Reading the Room**: If your context snapshot shows high pending tasks, JARVIS proactively suggests "Overwhelm Protocols" instead of more work.

## 🎨 Design System: Neon Nocturne
- **Manrope/Space Grotesk**: A return to premium, editorial-grade typography for maximum readability.
- **Glassmorphism**: A fully transparent, glass-like UI that layers over your goals.
- **Fluid Motion**: Slide-and-fade transitions for every dynamic layout shift.
